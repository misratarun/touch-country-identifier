Touch Country Identifier

A calm world map for iPad. Touch a country and it darkens briefly, like a pressed button on an AAC device. Then its name is spoken and its flag appears in a fixed corner.

In planning. No app code yet.

What it does

Touch a place → hear its name. That's the whole app. A parent or teacher does the teaching. The app has no quiz, scoring, prompts, praise sounds or levels.

Finger touches a country→ Country darkens briefly→ Name is spoken (waits its turn)→ Flag shows in the corner

Design choices

TopicChoice
ModelBehave like an AAC button grid: predictable, the same every time.
Press feedbackThe country darkens briefly, then goes back to normal.
Fast tapsNames queue, and each waits for the previous one to finish. None get cut off. The darken still happens instantly on every tap.
CoverageEvery country speaks, and they all behave the same way.
FlagShown in one fixed corner. It appears when its name starts playing and stays until the next name.
Text on screenNone.
OceanTapping the ocean is silent, like the empty space between buttons.
StimulationFixed muted colors. No animations except the darken, and no sounds except names.
Small countriesLarger invisible tap zones, so a fingertip can hit them on the first try.
OfflineEverything, audio included, downloads on install. After that, every tap responds instantly with or without a connection.

Voice clips are not included

The spoken names should be in the same voice the user already hears on their own AAC app. So the clips are recorded from that app and kept out of this repo. Many AAC voices are commercially licensed, which is another reason not to republish them.

The planned recording process:

  1. Turn on the iPad's screen recording with the microphone off.
  2. Have the AAC app speak each country name, leaving about 2 seconds of silence between names.
  3. Move the recording to a Mac. A script will split it at the silences into one clip per country.
The splitting script and a clip review page are still to come. These steps will be updated when they land.

Planned tech

  • Build: Vite, plain JavaScript and vite-plugin-pwa, with everything precached.
  • Map: Natural Earth 1:50m shapes from world-atlas, drawn as SVG with d3-geo and topojson-client.
  • Audio: Web Audio, with every clip decoded at startup and played through a first-in, first-out queue.
  • Flags: flag-icons (MIT).
  • Install: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) keeps the app on screen.
